About Xajon.

A beauty parlour and makeup academy led by Babli Sarma, located in Gopal Bazar, Nalbari, Assam.

Xajon brings together beauty and grooming services, hair care treatments, bridal and party makeup, and training options for people interested in learning beautician and makeup skills.

Whether you are enquiring about a service or exploring a course, contact us for current availability, pricing and further details.

A Few Helpful Answers.

For anything not covered here, please contact the parlour directly.

Are all service rates fixed?

No. Rates may vary by service, and bargaining may be possible. Please enquire for the current rate before booking.

Are occasional offers available?

Yes. Occasional offers may be announced through advertisements. Please check current advertisements or contact the parlour for active offers.

Is there any relaxation for multiple customers?

Yes. Please contact the parlour to enquire about available relaxation for multiple customers.

When can I join a course?

Admissions are available in summer and winter. The next batch is expected in November; please contact the academy to confirm the year, start date and availability.

How can I learn more about a course?

Call or WhatsApp either listed number to enquire about course details and admissions.
